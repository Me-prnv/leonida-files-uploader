Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Leonida Files Uploader ("the App") is a private tool operated by its single owner to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channels. The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the App accesses

  • Google account authorization: with your permission, the App receives an OAuth token for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope, which only allows uploading videos and setting their details and thumbnails.
  • The App does not read, collect or store your YouTube viewing data, comments, subscribers, analytics, contacts, or any other personal information.

How the data is used and stored

  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file, and is used only to upload videos the owner has approved.
  • No data is sent to any server operated by the App. There is no App server, database, analytics or advertising.
  • Video files and their titles and descriptions are created by the owner and sent only to YouTube.

Sharing

The App does not sell, share, or disclose any user data to third parties. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube through the YouTube API Services to perform uploads.

Cookies and device data

The App does not use cookies and does not store, access or collect information on any device or browser other than the local token file described above.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Deleting the local token file removes all data the App stores. The App deletes stored authorization data within 30 days after access is revoked or on request.
  • To request deletion or ask questions, email mailtopranavkhandelwal@gmail.com.
